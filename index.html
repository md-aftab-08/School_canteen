About Us

A Kitchen Filled with Love & Tradition

At Tandra's, we believe that food is more than just sustenance — it's a connection to home. Founded by Tandra Ma'am over a decade ago, our canteen has been the heart of student life, providing wholesome, home-cooked meals that taste just like they were made in your mother's kitchen.

Every dish is prepared fresh each morning using hand-picked ingredients, traditional recipes, and an unwavering commitment to hygiene and quality. Whether it's our aromatic dal, our signature chicken curry, or our beloved biryani — every bite is a warm hug from home.
